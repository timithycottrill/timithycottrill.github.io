AI YouTube Studio Automation connects the operator’s own media workflows, review queues, and authorized publishing accounts.

This is the public information site for a privately operated n8n and local-worker installation. It is not a public sign-in portal, and it does not provide access to channels or production files.

Organize production

Use selected spreadsheet rows and documents to organize scripts, approval decisions, media jobs, and publication records.

Prepare and review

Coordinate local media preparation and rendering, with durable job states and editorial review checkpoints.

Publish with authorization

Upload approved videos and manage their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists, and publication status on authorized channels.

Why Google access is used

Depending on the connected workflow, Google authorization lets the installation read and update selected Google Sheets review queues, work with selected Drive files or Docs, identify an authorized YouTube channel, and upload or manage its approved content. Google’s consent screen shows the permissions requested by each connection.

Account permissions and working automation features are separate: a granted permission does not mean every feature runs unattended. Campaign discovery, editorial review, and some platform submissions still require operator action.

Control stays with the account owner

Google sign-in takes place with Google. This website does not ask for passwords, API keys, or OAuth tokens. The operator can disconnect an n8n credential or revoke the app’s access in the Google Account settings.
